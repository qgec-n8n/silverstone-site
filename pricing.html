Pricing FAQs for UK small businesses

How do the setup fees and monthly retainers work?

The setup fee covers the build, integrations, testing and launch work. The monthly retainer covers monitoring, iteration, support and the ongoing automation layer once the system is live.

Should I start with a flagship pack or a niche atlas module?

Start with a flagship pack if you want the fastest route to a complete workflow. Use the niche atlas if you need a smaller fix first or want to compare multiple modules and bundles before you commit.

Can we start with one workflow and expand later?

Yes. Many clients begin with one high-leverage workflow, prove the ROI, then add the next module once the first system is paying back.

What happens in the automation audit?

We look at your current bottlenecks, identify the highest-value workflow to automate first, and recommend the pack or module that fits your stage, team and existing tools.

How quickly can a pricing pack go live?

Most focused systems can launch within one to two weeks after the audit, depending on your stack, approvals and how many moving parts need to be connected.
