Paperwork and no‑shows breaking the patient journey

When intake forms, reminders and follow‑ups are handled manually, it’s easy for patients to drop off before they finish a plan – and for clinicians to lose time to admin.

  • Paper or ad‑hoc digital intake forms that are incomplete, hard to read or misfiled before the first appointment.
  • Patients forgetting appointments or dropping out halfway through their treatment plan.
  • Clinicians or admin staff manually sending reminders and follow‑up messages between sessions.
  • No simple view of who is on track, who is overdue and who is at risk of dropping out.
  • Ongoing worries about keeping consent records and medical history organised and accessible.

Smart Intake & Rebooking Hub

A joined‑up patient‑journey system that captures information cleanly, keeps appointments front of mind and nudges patients to complete their plan.

What’s included

  • Digital intake forms that capture key history and consent before the first appointment.
  • Simple triage flows to route new enquiries to the right clinician or service.
  • Automated reminders for upcoming appointments, with options to reschedule where your tools allow.
  • Treatment‑plan reminder sequences that nudge patients to book follow‑up sessions at the right time.
  • Optional check‑in messages after key sessions to gather simple outcome or comfort feedback.

What this changes week to week

Clinicians start sessions with the right information in front of them and fewer gaps in their diaries. Patients are reminded gently when appointments are due and are less likely to drift away halfway through a plan.

Common questions from physio and chiro clinics

Can this integrate with our existing practice management system?

Often, yes. We regularly work alongside established practice systems rather than replacing them, using secure connections and careful scoping so the setup stays safe and manageable.

How is patient data kept secure?

We follow your existing policies on data handling, use encrypted tools where appropriate and avoid over‑collecting information. Specific data flows are agreed with you in advance.

Can we tailor reminders by condition or treatment type?

Where your systems support it, we can vary reminder timing and messaging by plan type so higher‑risk or longer programmes get the right level of support.
