When the phone never stops but the jobs slip through

Between crawling under sinks, driving between jobs and fetching parts, it’s easy for calls and messages to stack up. The result is missed emergencies, slow quotes and evenings buried in admin.

  • Calls going to voicemail while you’re under a sink, in a loft or driving between jobs.
  • Emergency work going to whichever competitor happens to pick up first.
  • Quotes sent but rarely chased, so good work quietly disappears.
  • Evenings spent sorting texts, voicemails and WhatsApp threads into a rough schedule for the next day.
  • No simple overview of which jobs are urgent, waiting on parts or ready to invoice.

Trades Virtual Office

A lean automation stack that behaves like a part‑time office admin: answering routine calls, sorting jobs and chasing quotes while you’re earning money on site.

What’s included

  • An AI receptionist that answers calls when you can’t, with clear messages and simple routing for emergencies.
  • Job‑triage questions that collect details like location, problem and urgency before you call back.
  • Quote‑chasing text flows so customers are politely nudged to approve or decline without extra phone tag.
  • Simple calendar and scheduling integrations so routine work drops neatly into your week.

Instead of guessing what’s in your voicemail each night, you’ll see a clean list of new jobs, emergency issues and quotes to chase. Routine follow‑ups happen automatically and your diary fills in a steadier, more predictable way.

Common questions from trades

Do I need a fancy job management system first?

Not necessarily. We can plug into popular job‑management tools where you have them, or start with simple calendars and spreadsheets if that’s what you’re using today. The goal is to improve your week, not force a replatform.

Will callers know they’re talking to an AI?

We keep the experience simple and human‑feeling. For many workflows the assistant gathers details and confirms basics, then hands off clearly to you or your team for anything nuanced.

How are emergencies handled differently from routine jobs?

We agree simple rules for emergency keywords and scenarios so those jobs are flagged and routed differently – for example, sending priority alerts while routine jobs wait for normal office hours.
