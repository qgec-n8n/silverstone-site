  • Portal leads and calls stack up whenever the team is out on viewings or valuations.

Estate Agent FAQs

Straight answers about how this works alongside your team and tools.

Will this replace my negotiators?

No. It handles the repetitive capture, booking and chasing so your negotiators spend more time winning instructions and progressing deals.

Does it work with our existing CRM and calendars?

Yes. We connect to the tools you already use—Outlook or Google calendars, your CRM and phone system—so you don’t need to rip anything out.

How long does it take to go live?

Most branches are live in a couple of weeks after the audit and playbook sign-off. We launch in stages to keep things controlled.

Will it sound robotic or scripted?

No. We base the wording on your own emails and brochures, then refine it with your team so replies feel like your branch.

What happens if something goes wrong or we want to change the journeys?

We monitor performance, add fallbacks and can adjust journeys quickly. You stay in control of approvals and we handle the edits.

Is this suitable for multi-branch agencies?

Yes. Routing rules can reflect territories and branch ownership so enquiries flow to the right team while keeping messaging consistent.
