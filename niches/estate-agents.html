Estate Agent Automation FAQs

Will this replace my negotiators?

No. The system answers quickly, books viewings and collects feedback so your negotiators spend more time in live conversations and closing deals.

Does it work with our existing CRM and calendars?

Yes. We connect to your email, calendars and preferred CRM so the automation layer works with your current stack instead of replacing it.

How long does it take to go live?

Most branches launch the core journeys within a few weeks after the automation audit and playbook sign-off.

Will it sound robotic or scripted?

We use your existing wording and brand guidelines so responses feel like your branch. Messages are human, concise and on-brand.

What happens if something goes wrong or we want to change the journeys?

We monitor performance, refine flows with your team and keep everything adjustable. You stay in control of messaging and escalation paths.

Is this suitable for multi-branch agencies?

Yes. We can configure branch-specific rules, calendars and handovers so enquiries route cleanly no matter how many locations you run.
