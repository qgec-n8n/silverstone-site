3.9 FAQs

Estate agent FAQs

Quick answers to the common questions branches ask before launching.

Will this replace my negotiators?

No. The automation handles first response, booking, and routine follow-up so negotiators can focus on valuations, viewings, and deals that need a human touch.

Which CRMs and calendars can you connect?

We integrate with popular estate agency CRMs plus shared calendars like Google and Microsoft 365. During onboarding we confirm the exact connectors and permissions required.

How fast can we go live?

Most branches see their first automated responses within two weeks. Complex routing or multi-branch rollouts may take longer, but we always provide a launch timeline.

What if a prospect wants to speak to a person?

Escalations are built in. When someone requests a call, valuation, or urgent update, the system routes it to the right negotiator immediately with full context.
