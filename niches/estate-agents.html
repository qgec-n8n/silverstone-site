The admin that quietly kills your instructions

When you’re plate‑spinning valuations, viewings and chasing solicitors, admin piles up in the background. Slow responses and missed calls quietly turn into lost instructions.

  • Phones ringing out while negotiators are on viewings or valuations, so hot vendors call the next agent on the list.
  • Portal and website enquiries stacking up in shared inboxes with slow or inconsistent responses.
  • Viewings booked but not confirmed, leading to no‑shows and wasted travel time.
  • Landlord onboarding that feels manual and error‑prone, with documents and AML checks scattered across systems.
  • Evenings spent catching up on email instead of progressing pipelines and deals.

“Never Miss a Viewing” pack

A simple automation stack that catches every portal lead, books viewings into the right calendars and keeps buyers warm while your negotiators stay in front of clients.

What’s included

  • 24/7 call and portal lead capture with branded SMS or WhatsApp follow‑ups when you miss a call.

Week to week, your negotiators see a clean list of new leads, booked viewings and follow‑ups due instead of digging through inboxes. Vendors and buyers are acknowledged quickly, viewings are confirmed automatically and your evenings become time to progress deals rather than chase admin.

Common questions from estate agents

How quickly can we go live?

It depends on your current tools and how many branches you have, but many agents are able to go live in around 2–4 weeks once we’ve agreed the flows and gained access to calendars and inboxes. We’ll map this out on your audit call.

Does this replace my negotiators or admin team?

No. The assistant handles repetitive tasks like lead capture, confirmations and reminders so your people can focus on valuations, viewings and progressing sales. You stay fully in control of conversations and decisions.

Can you plug into our existing CRM and portals?

In many cases we can integrate with common estate‑agency CRMs, email and calendar tools, or at least mirror their workflows. We’ll review your current setup and agree the safest, simplest way to connect things.
