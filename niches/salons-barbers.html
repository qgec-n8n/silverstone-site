Chair‑Filler Automation Suite

Common questions from salons and barbers

Will this replace our existing booking software?

No. The automations sit on top of your current booking system, using it as the source of truth. We connect to what you already use instead of forcing you onto a new platform.

How do we avoid annoying clients with too many messages?

We agree sensible cadences and tone up front, then test and refine. Messages are short, polite and useful – not constant sales pushes.

Can different stylists or services have different reminder rules?

Yes. Where your tools allow it, we can tailor reminder timing and wording by service type, stylist or location so high‑value work gets the right level of attention.
