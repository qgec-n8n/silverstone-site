Busy services, ringing phones and missed opportunities

When the bar is full or service is in full swing, phones and inboxes are easy to miss. Guests wait longer for answers, book with someone else or leave without sharing a great experience.

  • Phones ringing off the hook at busy times, leading to missed bookings and frustrated callers.
  • Guests asking the same questions repeatedly about parking, check‑in, menus or facilities.
  • Teams juggling multiple inboxes – email, social channels and online travel agents – without a clear system.
  • Review requests being sent ad‑hoc, so great experiences never turn into public feedback.
  • Limited visibility on the questions guests ask most often and where small fixes could improve their stay.

Common questions from hospitality teams

Will this replace our booking engine or channel manager?

No. The concierge works alongside your existing systems, using them as the source of truth for availability and bookings.

Can we control what the concierge is allowed to say?

Yes. We agree approved answers, topics and guardrails up front so the assistant stays firmly within your brand and policy.

How do we avoid double‑bookings?

We design flows so the concierge checks or hands off to your existing booking tools rather than inventing availability itself.
