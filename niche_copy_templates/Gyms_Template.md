# Gyms & Fitness Studios – Niche Landing Page

## 1. Niche Overview & Positioning

This niche covers independent gyms, functional fitness spaces and boutique studios. Most operate
on recurring memberships, class packs or a mix of both, with revenue heavily affected by member
churn and utilisation.

Owners often juggle coaching, front-of-house duties and admin. New members join
enthusiastically but many drift away within months if not supported properly. Automation helps
highlight at-risk members, trigger supportive nudges and run structured win-back campaigns
without adding pressure to the coaching team.

FAQs:


### Which membership systems can you work with?

We work with common membership and booking platforms used by independent gyms and
studios. Where direct integrations are not available, we use exports and simple connections to
keep journeys running.

### Will members feel like they are being spammed?

The focus is on supportive, low-volume communication that feels helpful, not pushy. You decide
how often members should receive messages and can adjust anything that does not feel right.

### Can we treat different membership types differently?

Yes. You can define different journeys for contract members, month-to-month members, class-
pack holders and other groups. This ensures messages and offers stay relevant.

### How do we measure success?

We track retention and engagement metrics such as churn rate, visit frequency and reactivation
numbers. These are summarised in simple reports so you can see whether things are improving.

### Will this create more work for coaches?

The aim is to reduce manual admin, not increase it. Coaches may be involved in shaping message
tone, but day-to-day the system handles the heavy lifting.

### Can we switch off or change journeys easily?

Yes. Journeys can be paused, edited or expanded as your membership model changes. You remain
in control of the approach.
