# Salons & Barbers – Niche Landing Page

## 1. Niche Overview & Positioning

This niche covers hair salons, barbershops and beauty studios with one or more chairs or
treatment rooms. Revenue depends on keeping columns full, keeping stylists and therapists busy
and encouraging clients to rebook regularly.

Most salons still rely on a mix of phone calls, walk-ins, online bookings and direct messages. No-
shows and last-minute cancellations are painful, and owners often spend evenings sending
reminders, moving bookings around and replying to messages. Automation helps by handling
reminders, rebooking and simple enquiries in the background so the team can focus on clients in
the chair.

### 3.11 FAQ Section

Section title:

FAQs:


### Will clients be annoyed by automated messages?

Clients are generally grateful for clear reminders and easy ways to change appointments. We work
with you to set sensible limits on how often messages go out and what they say. If you receive
feedback that something feels too much, we adjust it.

### Does this work with our current booking system?

In most cases, yes. We aim to connect to the system you already use so that bookings and changes
stay in one place. If direct integration is not possible, we design simple handovers that still keep
your diary accurate.

### Do we have to take deposits for this to work?

No. The suite works with or without deposits. That said, we can help you introduce deposits or
stricter cancellation rules if you decide they are appropriate for your clientele.

### How long does setup take?

For a typical independent salon, the core flows can usually be live within a few weeks. We start
with reminders and simple rebooking journeys, then expand once you are comfortable with how
everything feels.

### Can we send different messages for different stylists or services?

Yes. You can decide to treat high-value services or specific stylists differently. For example, colour
appointments might have earlier reminders or firmer cancellation language than a quick trim.

### What if a client replies with a complex request?

Whenever a client message falls outside the usual patterns, the system flags it for a human to
handle. We design these handovers so that nothing important gets missed.
