Representative client journeys

Anonymised snapshots of how our niche‑specific bundles work in practice. Use them as a guide to what an automation audit can unlock for you.

Estate agents – Never Miss a Viewing Pack

An independent agency layered a "Never Miss a Viewing" pack over their existing portals. An AI receptionist answers portal leads, asks a few qualification questions and books viewings into the team’s calendars while they’re on the road, instead of after hours.

Salons – Chair‑Filler Automation Suite

A small salon introduced automated reminders, rebooking prompts and simple no‑show back‑fill flows. Regulars now get gentle nudges at the right time, mid‑week gaps are easier to fill and the owner spends less time chasing appointments manually.

Trades – Trades Virtual Office

A local trades business switched on a "Trades Virtual Office" bundle. Urgent calls are triaged, quotes are chased automatically and evening admin is replaced by a simple summary of jobs to approve.

* These journeys are anonymised composites based on Silverstone’s target bundles. Swap in your own tools, platforms and processes during the audit.
