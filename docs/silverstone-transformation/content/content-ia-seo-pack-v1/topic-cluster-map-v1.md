## UK and London publishing rules

**Recommendation:**
- Use UK terminology, regulation and operational context where relevant.
- Do not add “London” to every title.
- Do not create borough pages from a shared template.
- A local page must contain verified address/service-area information, local delivery detail and genuinely distinct content.
- National service pages can link to one approved London page; industry pages should remain useful across the UK.

## Source and claim controls

Every article brief must carry:
- target reader and decision stage;
- primary question;
- source list;
- claims requiring evidence;
- service and industry links;
- human reviewer;
- update date;
- consolidation check against existing routes.

**Sourced fact:** Google recommends unique, helpful, people-first content and warns against making multiple URLs carry the same information.
