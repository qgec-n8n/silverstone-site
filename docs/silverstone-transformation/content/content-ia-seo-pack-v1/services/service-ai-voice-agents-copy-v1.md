## The problem this service addresses

Phone calls contain context that menus and voicemail often lose. A voice agent can support a defined conversational flow, but only when knowledge, action permissions and human handoff are designed explicitly.

## Outcomes to design for

- More consistent handling of routine calls
- Structured information captured before handoff
- Coverage for approved out-of-hours call types
- Clear escalation when a person should take over

These are design objectives, not promised results. Agree the baseline, owner and measurement method during discovery.

## What the service can include

- Call-flow design
- Knowledge and response boundaries
- Inbound or outbound voice workflows
- Qualification, routing and message capture
- Booking or CRM actions where appropriate
- Testing, monitoring and human handoff rules

The final scope should state what Silverstone owns, what the client owns, which systems are involved and how changes are approved.

## Common use cases

- Capturing missed or out-of-hours enquiries
- Qualifying routine inbound calls
- Confirming or requesting appointments
- Collecting job or property details
- Routing callers by approved business rules

## Delivery process

1. **Discovery:** Understand the current journey, users, systems and failure points.
2. **Definition:** Agree the first outcome, constraints, data, handoffs and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design:** Produce the content, flow, prototype or technical plan needed to test the approach.
4. **Build:** Implement the agreed release with clear ownership and change control.
5. **Validate:** Test normal, error, mobile, accessibility and exception paths appropriate to the service.
6. **Handover and review:** Document operation, support, monitoring and the next decision point.

## Fit and boundaries

**A useful fit**
- A repeated problem has an accountable owner.
- The people who perform the work can explain the current process.
- Relevant systems and policies can be reviewed.
- The business is willing to start with a defined scope.
- Success can be observed without inventing a target.

**Not included by default**
- Unsupervised decisions in sensitive situations
- Claims of human equivalence
- Use without consent, privacy and escalation review

## Questions buyers often ask

### Will callers know it is automated?

Disclosure and consent should be clear and appropriate to the flow.

### What happens when it does not understand?

The design needs retry limits, fallback wording, message capture and an escalation path.

### Can it make sensitive decisions?

Sensitive, unusual or regulated decisions should remain with an authorised person.
