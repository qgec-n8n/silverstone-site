## The problem this service addresses

Front-desk work is spread across phone, web chat, email, forms and booking tools. The useful product is not a generic chatbot; it is a controlled intake and routing layer based on the organisation’s actual rules.

## Outcomes to design for

- A more consistent first response
- Fewer routine enquiries left in separate inboxes
- Better message capture and routing
- A clearer handoff between automation and staff

These are design objectives, not promised results. Agree the baseline, owner and measurement method during discovery.

## What the service can include

- Phone, web or messaging intake
- FAQ and availability responses
- Message-taking and contact capture
- Appointment request or booking flows
- Escalation and exception handling
- Reporting and iterative improvement

The final scope should state what Silverstone owns, what the client owns, which systems are involved and how changes are approved.

## Common use cases

- Answering approved practical questions
- Taking structured messages
- Directing visitors to the right booking route
- Collecting minimum enquiry details
- Sending routine confirmations and follow-up

## Delivery process

1. **Discovery:** Understand the current journey, users, systems and failure points.
2. **Definition:** Agree the first outcome, constraints, data, handoffs and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design:** Produce the content, flow, prototype or technical plan needed to test the approach.
4. **Build:** Implement the agreed release with clear ownership and change control.
5. **Validate:** Test normal, error, mobile, accessibility and exception paths appropriate to the service.
6. **Handover and review:** Document operation, support, monitoring and the next decision point.

## Fit and boundaries

**A useful fit**
- A repeated problem has an accountable owner.
- The people who perform the work can explain the current process.
- Relevant systems and policies can be reviewed.
- The business is willing to start with a defined scope.
- Success can be observed without inventing a target.

**Not included by default**
- Clinical, legal or financial judgement
- A promise that every enquiry will convert
- Replacing essential human support

## Questions buyers often ask

### Is this intended to replace reception?

The service is positioned as support for repeatable first-response work, with people retaining judgement and exceptions.

### Will it use our existing booking system?

That depends on supported integration methods and a tested source of truth.

### What if a customer is upset or vulnerable?

The workflow should identify trigger phrases and route the interaction to a person rather than continue a standard script.
