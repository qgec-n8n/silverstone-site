## The problem this service addresses

Content becomes risky or generic when it is generated without approved source material, a named audience or an editorial owner. A useful system begins with expertise and provenance, then repurposes it deliberately.

## Outcomes to design for

- A consistent publishing rhythm
- Clearer reuse of one approved source across channels
- Content linked to services and buyer questions
- Less time lost starting every asset from scratch

These are design objectives, not promised results. Agree the baseline, owner and measurement method during discovery.

## What the service can include

- Content strategy and topic planning
- Interview or source-led drafting
- Website and landing-page copy
- Article, email and social repurposing
- Editorial review and claim checks
- Publishing workflow and performance review

The final scope should state what Silverstone owns, what the client owns, which systems are involved and how changes are approved.

## Common use cases

- Turning interviews into service-page copy
- Building a search-aware article cluster
- Repurposing one approved article into email and social posts
- Creating sales enablement from existing expertise
- Maintaining a review queue for regulated or sensitive content

## Delivery process

1. **Discovery:** Understand the current journey, users, systems and failure points.
2. **Definition:** Agree the first outcome, constraints, data, handoffs and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design:** Produce the content, flow, prototype or technical plan needed to test the approach.
4. **Build:** Implement the agreed release with clear ownership and change control.
5. **Validate:** Test normal, error, mobile, accessibility and exception paths appropriate to the service.
6. **Handover and review:** Document operation, support, monitoring and the next decision point.

## Fit and boundaries

**A useful fit**
- A repeated problem has an accountable owner.
- The people who perform the work can explain the current process.
- Relevant systems and policies can be reviewed.
- The business is willing to start with a defined scope.
- Success can be observed without inventing a target.

**Not included by default**
- Fabricated expertise, clients or results
- Volume publishing without quality control
- Medical, legal or regulated claims without review

## Questions buyers often ask

### Will the content sound generic?

Not when the process starts from interviews, source documents, examples and an agreed voice rather than prompts alone.

### Can content be published automatically?

Drafting and workflow steps may be automated, but publication authority should match risk and brand requirements.

### How much content do we need?

Enough to answer priority buyer questions and support the commercial structure. Volume is not the objective.
