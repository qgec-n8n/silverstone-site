## The problem this service addresses

A website can look polished and still leave visitors unsure what the business does, who it is for or what to do next. The work starts by reducing that uncertainty, not by choosing animation or a component library.

## Outcomes to design for

- A clearer offer and page hierarchy
- A simpler route to enquiry or booking
- Search-ready page structure and metadata
- A maintainable website your team can update

These are design objectives, not promised results. Agree the baseline, owner and measurement method during discovery.

## What the service can include

- Discovery and audience mapping
- Information architecture and conversion copy
- Responsive interface design
- Front-end development and integrations
- Technical SEO foundations
- Testing, launch support and handover

The final scope should state what Silverstone owns, what the client owns, which systems are involved and how changes are approved.

## Common use cases

- Rebuilding an outdated brochure site
- Creating a conversion path for a new service
- Separating service and industry content
- Improving forms, booking and lead handoff
- Migrating content without losing useful URLs

## Delivery process

1. **Discovery:** Understand the current journey, users, systems and failure points.
2. **Definition:** Agree the first outcome, constraints, data, handoffs and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design:** Produce the content, flow, prototype or technical plan needed to test the approach.
4. **Build:** Implement the agreed release with clear ownership and change control.
5. **Validate:** Test normal, error, mobile, accessibility and exception paths appropriate to the service.
6. **Handover and review:** Document operation, support, monitoring and the next decision point.

## Fit and boundaries

**A useful fit**
- A repeated problem has an accountable owner.
- The people who perform the work can explain the current process.
- Relevant systems and policies can be reviewed.
- The business is willing to start with a defined scope.
- Success can be observed without inventing a target.

**Not included by default**
- Guaranteed rankings or traffic
- Unapproved claims, reviews or portfolio items
- Open-ended redesign without an agreed scope

## Questions buyers often ask

### Do we need a complete rebuild?

Not automatically. The recommendation should follow an audit of the current content, platform, maintenance burden and conversion path.

### Can you make us rank first?

No ethical provider can promise a position. The service can implement sound technical and content foundations, then measure performance.

### Will we be able to edit it?

The scope should name the content owner, editing workflow and handover requirements before build decisions are final.
