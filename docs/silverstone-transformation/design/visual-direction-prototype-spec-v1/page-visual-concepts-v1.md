## 1. Home page `/`

### Concept: “From friction to flow”
The home page is the most cinematic route, but it remains understandable before enhancement loads.

### Wireframe
1. **Hero:** left-aligned proposition and two conventional CTAs; right side shows an original constellation of six capability nodes connected to one central human-control ring. A restrained shader may sit behind the visual stage only.
2. **Value strip:** four text-led outcomes with Machined Signal icons.
3. **Problem selector:** three large choices — customer response, digital product, repeated operations — which reveal relevant services without changing route.
4. **Six-service field:** asymmetric 2+4 composition, not six identical cards. Voice, receptionist and automation share a connected lower band; web, app and content retain distinct visual frames.
5. **Demo: enquiry-to-handoff:** explicit-start synthetic scenario showing capture, rule check, booking option and human escalation.
6. **Industries atlas:** nine labelled pathways grouped by operating pattern rather than aesthetic stereotype.
7. **Work with your existing tools:** native, user-controlled compatibility rail.
8. **Process:** Understand → Design → Build and improve, represented as three anchored chambers with decision gates.
9. **Qualification:** “A good fit starts with a repeated problem” checklist.
10. **Evidence:** approved case-study slot or omission.
11. **Decision rail:** Services, Industries, How we work; static cards replace the old lower carousel.
12. **Final CTA:** graphite conversion chamber.

### Motion
Hero sequence 680ms maximum after static content is visible. Node lines draw once; no perpetual orbit. Demo never autoplays.

### Estate Agents `/industries/estate-agents`
**Signature:** property-enquiry switchboard. A valuation request, portal enquiry and viewing request enter separate lanes; vendor/landlord updates are visibly human-owned. Use property-card geometry, map pin and calendar nodes without luxury-house clichés.

### Hospitality `/industries/hospitality`
**Signature:** guest-journey service bell. Availability, practical questions and changes move around a central reservation record; allergy, accessibility, complaint and emergency branches turn visibly to staff. Use warm documentary venue imagery only when approved.

### Salons & Barbers `/industries/salons-barbers`
**Signature:** chair-and-calendar availability weave. Service duration, practitioner, deposit and cancellation rules assemble into a booking slot; suitability questions stop at practitioner handoff.

### Trades & Home Services `/industries/trades`
**Signature:** job-intake dispatch board. Location, urgency, job type and photo-request nodes create a callback/slot request; attendance, price and safety outcomes remain unconfirmed until staff/system approval.

### eCommerce Brands `/industries/ecommerce`
**Signature:** order-state conveyor. Product question, order status, return and post-purchase messages move between store, helpdesk, fulfilment and email. The visual uses labelled states, not endless moving boxes.

### Physio & Chiropractic Clinics `/industries/physios-chiropractors`
**Signature:** non-clinical intake boundary. Contact, clinic preference and high-level reason flow to booking/preparation; clinical, urgent or out-of-scope content immediately enters a human/urgent-care guidance boundary. Avoid anatomical gimmicks.

### Dental Practices `/industries/dentists`
**Signature:** patient-admin recall orbit. New-patient enquiry, recall, form and diary-change paths surround a protected clinical-decision core that automation cannot enter.

### Gyms & Fitness Studios `/industries/gyms-fitness-studios`
**Signature:** trial-to-membership roster. Location/class preference, booking, consent and follow-up create a transparent sequence; injuries, disputes and sensitive issues divert to staff. Avoid body-transformation imagery.

### Fitness Coaches `/industries/fitness-coaches`
**Signature:** DM-to-consultation pathway. Message, fit/readiness questions and resource/consultation choice produce a concise handoff summary; health disclosures and unsuitable requests remain unassessed automatically.
