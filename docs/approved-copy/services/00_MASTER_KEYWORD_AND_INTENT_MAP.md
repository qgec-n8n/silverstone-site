## Ownership enforcement

- A service page may mention an adjacent term descriptively, but may not use it as its SEO title, H1 or repeated exact-match anchor.
- “AI agency UK” is not a secondary keyword for every service page.
- Industry modifiers belong to the corresponding vertical page. Service pages link down with natural anchors rather than inserting nine sector keyword lists.
- “Best”, “leading” and “top” are not self-declarations on service pages.
