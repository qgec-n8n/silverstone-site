## Prohibited claims

- Any metric described as a Silverstone client result without a named, approved case study.
- Guaranteed conversion, revenue, savings, ROI, ranking, response time or delivery date.
- “Average Silverstone result”, “typical outcome” or equivalent.
- Combining figures from different source rows into a synthetic case study.
- Removing the disclaimer or placing it so far from the figures that the relationship becomes unclear.

## Claims requiring owner approval

- Client logos, names, testimonials or deployment screenshots.
- Specific tool partnerships, certifications or accreditations.
- IP ownership transfer terms.
- Fixed support periods, warranties, prices, delivery times or no-lock-in terms.
- London office/address claims in service copy beyond the verified sitewide entity data.
- Regulatory-compliance promises or statements that a build is “GDPR compliant” without scoped legal and technical review.
