## Canonical and alias controls

- Canonicals must use `https://silverstone-ai.com` plus the route in this pack.
- Preserve permanent redirects from `/services/ai-agents-automation` and `/services/website-design-development`.
- Do not canonicalise industry pages to horizontal service pages; they must be genuinely distinct and self-canonical.
- Review Search Console after indexation. When two pages consistently rank for the same transactional family, compare intent satisfaction before consolidating or re-differentiating.
