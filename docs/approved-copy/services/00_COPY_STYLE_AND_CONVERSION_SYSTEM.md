# Copy Style and Conversion System

## Silverstone voice

- Calm, exact and commercially literate.
- Premium through judgement and restraint, not ornamental adjectives.
- Technical enough to expose architecture and risk, but always translated into buyer consequences.
- British English throughout.
- Confident about process; conservative about results.

## CTA hierarchy

1. **Primary:** `Book a discovery call` → `/book`.
2. **Supporting reassurance:** state what will be discussed, that the call is exploratory, and what the buyer should prepare.
3. **Commercial detail:** link to `/pricing` where scope/investment is the likely objection.
4. **Low-pressure route:** `/contact` may appear once near the final CTA, never as a competing hero button.

The Aether route-entry button opens the page body; it is not a conversion CTA.

## Benchmark presentation

- Label the unit as “Published benchmark”, “External benchmark” or “Illustrative benchmark evidence”.
- Show the exact value and a plain-English interpretation.
- Render the mandatory disclaimer with every metric group.
- Never use “our results”, “average Silverstone outcome” or “you will achieve”.
- Avoid presenting large outliers without their source-page context.

## Objection handling

- Answer fit, integration, control, risk, ownership, timeline uncertainty and what happens after launch.
- State poor-fit situations. Selectivity increases trust and protects delivery quality.
- Do not invent fixed timelines, prices, guarantees or certifications.
- Use FAQs as visible content, not as a schema tactic.

## Premium positioning rules

- Prefer “designed around”, “engineered”, “governed”, “scoped” and “measured” to generic innovation language.
- “Bespoke” is most natural on web and app pages.
- “Premium” is permitted where it describes craft or positioning, especially web/product work; it should not be repeated as a substitute for evidence.
- “Luxury” is excluded from public service copy.
- “Industry-leading”, “best”, “top” and “#1” require independent evidence and are prohibited in these pages.

## Prohibited clichés

Do not use: unlock your potential; harness the power of AI; cutting-edge solutions; revolutionise your business; game-changing; seamless transformation; future-proof your business; supercharge growth; next-level; one-size-fits-all.

## Page-differentiation system

- Web: buyer journey and commercial publishing system.
- App: product decisions, states and first-release discipline.
- Voice: real-time conversation engineering and telephony governance.
- Reception: front-desk operating model across channels.
- Content: source-to-channel editorial operations.
- Automation: orchestration, exception handling and observability.
- Consulting: decision quality, prioritisation and governance.

Each page must use its own metaphor set, process labels, objections and CTA setup. Shared facts—such as human oversight—must be expressed through the operational reality of that service.

## Consistency rules

- One H1.
- One dominant CTA.
- Short paragraphs and descriptive headings.
- No unsupported proof.
- No keyword lists in public copy.
- Essential copy must be prerendered.
- All demo surfaces must state whether they are live, synthetic or reserved.
