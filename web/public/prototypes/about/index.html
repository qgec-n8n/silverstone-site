About Silverstone

We build calm software, with a person in control.

Silverstone designs and builds web, product and automation work that keeps a human accountable for the signal. This page sets out our principles and our boundaries — not a head-count or a wall of logos.

What we do

A studio for honest operating software

Most businesses do not need more software. They need the software they already have to behave — to answer the routine question, capture the enquiry, and hand the rest to a person who can decide. That is the work we take on.

We start with one repeated problem and the smallest useful step. We build it so it is fast, accessible and easy to maintain, and we are explicit about where automation stops and judgement begins. When something is outside the agreed rules, it escalates to a person rather than guessing.

We would rather tell you a thing will not work than sell you a thing that will not work. That principle shapes how we scope, how we build and how we report.

Principles

Four operating principles

01

A human stays in control

Software escalates the decisions it cannot clearly own. People remain accountable.

02

Smallest useful step

We prove value with one focused build before expanding scope.

03

Honest by default

No invented metrics, no guaranteed rankings, no promises we cannot keep.

04

Accessible and fast

Keyboard, reduced motion and performance are requirements, not extras.

Decision ledger

What we automate, review or decline

A simple test we apply to any piece of work before we agree to build it.

Automate

Routine, well-defined, low-risk tasks with a clear right answer — capture, routing, status, reminders.

Review

Tasks that need a person to check before acting — anything ambiguous, sensitive or high-impact.

Decline

Work where automation would be unsafe or dishonest — clinical, legal or emergency judgement, or unbounded autonomy.

● If a task cannot be made safe and honest, we say no — and explain why.
Method

How we keep ourselves honest

  • Every boundary is written down and signed off
  • Every prototype is tested by keyboard and with reduced motion
  • Every release ships with monitoring and a documented rollback
  • Every review records what we kept, changed or stopped
