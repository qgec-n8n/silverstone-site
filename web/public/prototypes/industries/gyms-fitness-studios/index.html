Where friction appears

Interest fades fast. A trial that turns into a membership needs a visible next step at every stage — and a person for anything to do with safety or money.

    What this demonstrates — and what it does not

    Demonstrates

    • Sorting an intake into the right lane
    • Preparing a summary for a person to confirm
    • Routing the sensitive case to a human boundary

    Does not

    • Confirm, book or decide anything automatically
    • Use real customer data or live integrations
    • Cross the human-in-control boundary

    Static fallback: an intake is captured and sorted into a lane; routine requests reach a prepared summary for a person to confirm, and the sensitive request is routed to the front desk.
