Operating-pattern atlas

Find your business by how it actually operates.

We group nine sectors by operating pattern — appointment-led, enquiry-led, transaction-led and relationship-led — because the workflow matters more than the label. Each sector has one canonical instrument and a clear human-in-control boundary.

Appointment-led

Appointment-led sectors

A booking or appointment is the unit of work. The instrument assembles the slot and prepares it for a person to confirm.

Guest-journey service bell Hospitality Routine availability questions interrupt the floor when they could be answered around the booking. Chair-and-calendar availability weave Salons & barbers A short slot is booked for a long service, so the day runs late from the first appointment. Non-clinical intake boundary Physio & chiropractic clinics Symptom questions arrive where booking lives, and a non-clinical reply is a risk no clinic should take. Patient-admin recall orbit Dental practices Six-month recalls depend on someone remembering, so gaps appear in the diary and in care. Trial-to-membership roster Gyms & fitness studios A first class with no clear next step rarely becomes a membership.
Enquiry-led

Enquiry-led sectors

An enquiry is sorted into the right lane and prepared for a callback — with sensitive relationships kept human.

Property-enquiry switchboard Estate agents Portal leads, web forms and calls land in different places, so the same person is contacted twice — or missed entirely. Job-intake dispatch board Trades & home services A call that rings out while you are on a job is a quote that goes to the next number on the list.
Transaction-led

Transaction-led sectors

Messages meet a labelled order state. The routine is drafted; money and disputes stay a human decision.

Order-state conveyor eCommerce brands The same status question repeats hundreds of times while harder messages wait behind it.
Relationship-led

Relationship-led sectors

The first conversation is organised into a clean handoff, with anything personal or health-related left to a person.

DM-to-consultation pathway Fitness coaches A promising message gets buried, and the lead cools before a consultation is offered.
Human in control

One safeguard runs through every sector

Across all nine instruments, automation captures, sorts and prepares — and the sensitive case (safety, money, clinical judgement or a personal relationship) always reaches a named person. No instrument decides on its own.
