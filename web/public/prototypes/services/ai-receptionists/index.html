AI receptionists

One front desk for phone, web and messaging.

Enquiries arrive everywhere — calls, web chat, messages. The receptionist console brings them into one intake queue, then branches each to a clear destination: an approved answer, a booking, a captured message, or a person.

Four destinations

Where an enquiry goes

Every enquiry, from any channel, resolves to one of four destinations. The receptionist only answers from content you have approved.

Answer

A direct reply from approved content — hours, location, simple FAQs.

Booking

A slot is offered and held; a person confirms before it is final.

Message

Details are captured cleanly and routed to the right inbox.

Person

Anything sensitive or urgent goes straight to a named human.

Front-desk routing

Choose a channel and an enquiry type. The console takes the enquiry through intake, an approved-answer check and a routing decision to one of four destinations. Sensitive enquiries cannot be automated.

    Fit

    Where this fits

    • Enquiries arrive across several channels and get missed
    • Many questions are repetitive and have approved answers
    • You want sensitive matters to always reach a person
    Approved answers only. The receptionist answers from content you have signed off and routes everything else. It does not improvise policy, give regulated advice, or handle emergencies.
    FAQ

    Common questions

    Does it work across all our channels?

    It is designed to. Phone, web chat and messaging converge into one intake queue, so an enquiry is handled the same way wherever it starts.

    What about sensitive or urgent enquiries?

    They override routing and go straight to a named human. The console hands off rather than guessing.

    Can it confirm a booking on its own?

    It offers and holds a slot, but a person confirms before it is final. Automation never finalises the decision.
