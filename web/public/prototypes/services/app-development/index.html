Role boundaries

Three roles, one record

User

Sees only their own request and its status. Simple, fast, and impossible to break the data behind it.

Staff

Reviews, decides and progresses work. The decisions that matter stay with a person.

System

Holds one authoritative record, with history, so every view agrees on what happened.
